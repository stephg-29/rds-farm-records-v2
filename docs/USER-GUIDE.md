# Farm Records: quick guide

Farm Records keeps your stock, treatments, chemicals, paddock work and map in one place, on your phone, and works with no signal. Everything you record is saved on the phone first and sent to your farm's records when there's signal.

## Getting started

- Open the link Rural Data Services gave you and sign in. Then add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share, Add to Home Screen; Android: Install app). After that it opens anywhere, signal or not.
- The line under the farm name on Home says whether everything has been sent ("All synced"), or how many records are waiting for signal. Tap it to send now.
- Don't sign out while records are waiting to send; the app will tell you.

## Setting up (owner)

1. **More, Properties and paddocks:** each property with its PIC, then its paddocks.
2. **Map:** zoom to the property, tap ✎, Set start view. Draw each paddock's boundary (tap the corners, tap the first corner to finish). Add fences (coloured by energiser unit), pipes, troughs, gates.
3. **Stock, Add mob:** each mob with a starting count, class and paddock.
4. **Records, Chemicals:** your products with label WHP/ESI, then what's in the shed.
5. **More, Modules:** switch off anything you don't use; it only hides it.
6. **More, People:** invite staff and contractors (they get an email to set a password).

## Everyday

- **Move:** open a mob, Move, tap the paddock, count through the gate. If the count's out, choose Recount later or Accept and say why. Several mobs in one paddock: Move all. Or on the map, tap a mob then a paddock.
- **Treat:** open a mob, Treat. The withhold dates fill in from the label; the mob shows "Under withhold" until the day after they end.
- **Sold, died, bought:** open the mob: Sold or left, Deaths, Add stock. Take a photo of the NVD. Selling inside a withhold warns you first.
- **Split, merge, wean:** from the mob. If the mob's under withhold, you choose whether it carries over.
- **Report a problem:** Map, the orange +. GPS, paddock and the nearest trough or fence fill in; add photos.
- **Spray, fertiliser, feed, rain, vehicles, documents:** Records.
- **Made a mistake?** Open the record (from the mob's History, or Records) and change it or delete it. Every earlier version is kept, with who changed it and when.

## Reports

Records, Reports: the livestock reconciliation for the financial year (for the accountant), and the LPA audit pack (print, save as PDF, or spreadsheets). These are a record of what you entered; check them against current LPA requirements.
